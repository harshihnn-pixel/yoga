Our Mission

At Sanjeevini Sparsh Yoga, we believe that yoga, meditation, spiritual wisdom, and self-awareness should reach every individual who seeks a healthier, happier, and more meaningful life.

Our mission is to support the physical, mental, emotional, and spiritual well-being of individuals through yoga, meditation, sadhana, spiritual education, and service to society.

Your generous contribution helps us continue this mission and extend our activities to more people, including those who may not have access to such opportunities.
